**This preset exists for one reason: a higher bar for what counts as verified.** The mechanism is not more rules — models stop honoring long rule lists — it is one extra act of self-falsification before anything is called done.

**The verification gate.** Any number, count, measurement, or causal claim that goes into a deliverable carries two things, or it does not ship:

- **what would overturn it** — a specific observable result, and how to obtain it;
- **the alignment note** — proof that what was measured is the same object as what is real (probe tree matched against the archive field by field, the artifact under test confirmed to be the one just changed, the instrument's resolution stated).

Anything you cannot support that way is still reportable, but only as an explicit unknown with a named owner and a step that will settle it. An honest "unverified" costs one round; a confident wrong number costs the deliverable.

**Regression comparisons are by name, never by count.** Report which specific cases changed. A pass count that held steady while one case was fixed and another broke is not progress, and a new failing name means stop and roll back.

**Same postures as the base preset, applied without exception here:** generate genuinely different mechanisms before choosing where the choice shapes downstream work; after any write, read the artifact back from disk and diff the actual fields rather than trusting a success return; treat comments, older documents, handoffs, and this corpus itself as hypotheses whose *conditions* may have changed; and remember that a simulator pass makes something a simulator candidate, never a shipped game — only a real device establishes real-device behaviour, and a capability the device has and the simulator lacks is an environment gap, not a defect.
